## Purpose

A partner wants to tell the other something that bothers them, from a small habit to something said in anger, so both understand each other without blame. Bisou guides the words into one concrete issue, makes every note wait before it can be sent, lets the partner open it only when ready, and leaves the talk itself to the couple. It builds on the [journal](journal.md), [notifications](notifications.md) and the [installed app](pwa.md).

## Actors

- **Writer**: the partner who wrote a given note.
- **Recipient**: the writer's partner.
- **Outsider**: anyone else, unpaired people included.

## Running example

Sơn and Linh are a couple. On 12 November 2026 Linh came home at 22:00 without telling Sơn, and Sơn is upset.

- 12 November, 22:30: Sơn writes a note and saves it.
- 13 November, 06:30: the 8 hours have passed; Sơn rereads the note, changes one part and sends it; Linh's phone shows "Sơn có một lời nhắn nhỏ" at once.
- 13 November, 06:35: Sơn spots a typo in the sent note and fixes it; the note still shows "Chưa mở", nothing new reaches Linh and the 8 hours are not restarted.
- 13 November, 20:00: Linh opens it with "Mình sẵn sàng nghe" and reads the 06:35 version; 20:15: Linh closes it and the note appears in the journal for both.
- 13 November, 20:30: Linh edits her own line in the journal entry; Sơn sees the new line.
- Had Linh not opened it, one reminder would come at 07:00 on 16 November (the third Vietnam day after the send day).

## 1. Writing

A partner writes a note about one thing, in four short parts. Bisou may point out a blaming or generalising phrase but never stops the writer.

Example: at 22:30 on 12 November Sơn opens Lời nhắn nhỏ in the Journal tab and writes to Linh. What happened: "Em lúc nào cũng về muộn mà không nhắn anh." I feel: "Anh lo, rồi bực." Because to me: "Anh cần biết em về an toàn." I hope: "Lần sau về muộn em nhắn anh một câu nhé." A soft hint shows under the first part about "lúc nào cũng"; Sơn saves without changing it.

- R1. A note has one writer and one recipient, the writer's partner; only a partner in a couple can write one, and there is no limit on how many are written or wait (D9).
- R2. A note has four parts, in this order: "Chuyện đã xảy ra" ("What happened", one concrete time), "Mình thấy" ("I feel"), "Vì với mình" ("Because to me", the need) and "Mình mong" ("I hope", one concrete, doable request) (D2).
- R3. Each part is required and holds 1 to 500 characters, outer spaces ignored, line breaks kept; a note is text only, with no photos (G5).
- R4. A note is about one issue; the form asks for it, and Bisou does not judge whether it is (D2).
- R5. A part holding a generalising or blaming phrase from a fixed Vietnamese and English list ("lúc nào cũng", "luôn luôn", "không bao giờ", "tại anh", "tại em", "always", "never", "your fault") shows a soft hint suggesting one concrete time or "mình" wording; the hint never blocks saving or sending, and Bisou never rewrites the text (D3).
- R6. Notes live in a "Lời nhắn nhỏ" ("Kind notes") section of the Journal tab, where letters were; Bisou gets no new tab, and Home shows only the tile in R19 (G4).
- R7. Notes bring no new visual style; they reuse what Bisou already looks like (G10).
- R8. Notes never touch the garden: writing, sending or closing one changes no card, plant, topic or freshness (D11).

- AC1 (R1). Given Sơn and Linh are a couple, when Sơn writes a note, then its recipient is Linh with no other choice; a person not yet paired has no Lời nhắn nhỏ section.
- AC2 (R2, R3). Given Sơn leaves one part empty or only spaces, or types 501 characters in one part, then saving is refused; four parts of 1 to 500 characters save, and no photo can be added.
- AC3 (R5). Given Sơn types "Em lúc nào cũng về muộn", then a soft hint shows under that part; when Sơn saves without changing it, then the note saves exactly as typed.
- AC4 (R6, R8). Given the PO opens Bisou, then the tab bar is unchanged, notes are reached from the Journal tab, and the garden looks the same before and after a note is written, sent and closed.

## 2. Cool-down and reread

Every note waits 8 hours after it is first saved, small things included. Then the writer rereads it next to one question and decides to send, change or delete it.

Example: the note saved at 22:30 shows Sơn "Gửi được từ 06:30" ("Can be sent from 06:30"). At 23:10 Sơn opens it and cannot send it yet. At 06:30 on 13 November Sơn opens it and reads "Nếu một người bạn quý cả hai đứa đọc lời nhắn này, họ sẽ thấy gì?" above the send button, changes the first part to "Tối qua em về lúc 22:00 mà không nhắn anh trước." and sends.

- R9. A note can be sent only once 8 hours have passed since it was first saved, for every note; until then the writer sees the time from which it can be sent ("Gửi được từ 06:30") (D4).
- R10. Before it is sent a note is the writer's alone: the writer may read it, change it under R2 to R5 or delete it at any time, and a change does not restart the 8 hours: written 22:30 and changed at 23:10, it can still be sent from 06:30 (S12).
- R11. Once the 8 hours have passed, sending happens only from the reread: the note shows in full with the question "Nếu một người bạn quý cả hai đứa đọc lời nhắn này, họ sẽ thấy gì?" ("If a friend who cares about you both read this note, what would they see?") above the send button; the writer may still change it there (D4).
- R12. An unsent note never sends itself and never expires; it waits until the writer sends or deletes it.
- R13. Deleting an unsent note, after confirming, removes it for good; the partner never learns it existed.

- AC5 (R9). Given the note was saved at 22:30 on 12 November, then Sơn cannot send it at 06:29 on 13 November and can from 06:30; until then it shows "Gửi được từ 06:30".
- AC6 (R10). Given Sơn changes the note at 23:10, then it can still be sent from 06:30.
- AC7 (R11). Given it is 06:30, when Sơn opens the note to send it, then the question shows above the send button, and Sơn can change a part before sending.
- AC8 (R12, R13). Given Sơn never sends the note, then on 30 November it still waits unsent and Linh has seen nothing of it; when Sơn deletes it and confirms, then it is gone for good.

## 3. Sending, changing and withdrawing

A sent note reaches the recipient at once. Until the recipient opens it, the writer can still fix it, or take it back without a trace.

Example: at 06:30 Sơn sends; the note shows Sơn "Chưa mở". At 06:35 Sơn fixes a typo; Linh sees nothing new. Had Sơn withdrawn it at 06:40, Linh's Home and Lời nhắn nhỏ would look as before 06:30 and Linh's phone would already have shown the arrival message, and no reminder would come.

- R14. Sending delivers the note to the recipient at once; until the recipient opens it, the writer may still change it under R2 to R5, and nobody else can; a change after sending does not restart the 8 hours and sends no notification (S13; R58).
- R15. Until the recipient opens it, the writer may withdraw a sent note after confirming; it is gone for good for both, leaves the recipient's count and section, and any notification or reminder not yet sent is dropped; a notification already shown stays, and tapping it opens Lời nhắn nhỏ without the note (D6).
- R16. Once opened, a note can no longer be changed, withdrawn or deleted by the writer or anyone; after closing, the journal rules in R33 apply (D6, S13).
- R17. The writer sees each sent note as "Chưa mở" ("Not opened yet") or "Đã mở" ("Opened"), never a date, time or number of days (G7, A3).
- R18. The writer sees their own unsent and sent notes, most recently written first; the recipient never sees the writer's list.

- AC9 (R14, R17). Given Sơn sent the note at 06:30, then Sơn sees "Chưa mở" with no date or days, and can still change it until Linh opens it.
- AC10 (R15). Given Sơn withdraws the note at 06:40 and confirms, then Linh's Home and Lời nhắn nhỏ show nothing of it, and no reminder comes on 16 November.
- AC11 (R16). Given Linh opened the note at 20:00, then Sơn can no longer change, withdraw or delete it.

## 4. What the recipient sees before opening

The recipient knows only that a note from the partner waits: Home shows how many, Lời nhắn nhỏ shows a closed note with one button, nothing else.

Example: from 06:30 on 13 November Linh's Home shows the inbox tile with 1 and a moving mailbox; on Linh's phone set to reduce motion, the mailbox stays still. In Lời nhắn nhỏ Linh sees one note from Sơn and "Mình sẵn sàng nghe", with no part, date or length. Sơn's Home shows no tile.

- R19. While at least one sent note waits unopened for a partner, that partner's Home shows an inbox tile with the count of unopened notes and a moving mailbox; tapping it opens Lời nhắn nhỏ; with none waiting there is no tile, so Linh's is there from 06:30 to 20:00 on 13 November and absent before and after (D7, G4, S14).
- R20. When the phone asks for reduced motion, the mailbox stays still and nothing else changes (G8, A4).
- R21. Notes a partner sent never count on that partner's own Home.
- R22. A waiting note shows the recipient only that it comes from the partner, with the button "Mình sẵn sàng nghe" ("I'm ready to listen"): no part, label, date or length (G1, D5).
- R23. Lời nhắn nhỏ shows the recipient waiting notes first, oldest sent first, then opened notes not yet closed, oldest opened first.

- AC12 (R19, R21). Given it is 06:30 on 13 November, then Linh's Home shows the tile with 1 and the moving mailbox, and Sơn's Home shows no tile; given Sơn sends a second note, then Linh's tile shows 2.
- AC13 (R20). Given Linh's phone asks for reduced motion, then the mailbox stays still and the tile still shows 1.
- AC14 (R22). Given the note waits, then Linh sees only that a note from Sơn waits and "Mình sẵn sàng nghe", nothing of its parts, length or day.

## 5. Opening

The recipient opens a note only on purpose, when ready. There is no deadline and nothing opens by itself.

Example: at 20:00 on 13 November Linh taps the tile, then "Mình sẵn sàng nghe", and reads the four parts as Sơn sent them. Linh's tile goes, and Sơn sees "Đã mở".

- R24. The recipient sees a note's parts only after tapping "Mình sẵn sàng nghe" on it; a note never opens by itself and has no deadline (D5).
- R25. A note is opened once: from then it leaves the count, and the writer sees "Đã mở" (R17).
- R26. An opened note shows the recipient the writer's display name and the four parts, and the recipient can read it again until closing it.

- AC15 (R24). Given the note waits, then its parts stay hidden until Linh taps "Mình sẵn sàng nghe"; given Linh never taps it, then on 30 November it still waits.
- AC16 (R25, R26). Given Linh opens the note at 20:00, then Linh's tile goes, Sơn sees "Đã mở", and Linh can read it again before closing it.

## 6. Closing

After reading, the recipient closes the note with one short answer. The real conversation happens in person, not in Bisou.

Example: at 20:15 Linh picks "Mình muốn nói trực tiếp", adds "Mình hiểu là anh lo cho em, chứ không phải giận em." and closes the note. They talk over tea.

- R27. The recipient closes a note by picking exactly one of "Mình hiểu rồi, sẽ thử" ("I understand, I'll try"), "Mình muốn nói trực tiếp" ("I'd like to talk in person") or "Mình thấy khác, mình nói chuyện nhé" ("I see it differently, let's talk"), and may add one line after "Mình hiểu là..." ("What I understand is..."), up to 500 characters (G2, S15).
- R28. There is no deadline to close; an opened note stays in Lời nhắn nhỏ until the recipient closes it.
- R29. Closing is final as an act: the note cannot be reopened or closed again; the pick and the line afterwards change only as part of the journal entry (R33).
- R30. Bisou has no reply, thread, reaction or comment on a note beyond the pick and the line; the couple talks in person, as for the garden ([garden](garden.md) R16) (D1, D12).
- R31. The writer sees the pick and the line in the closed note's journal entry (G2, R32).

- AC17 (R27). Given Linh tries to close without a pick, then it is refused; one pick without a line closes; a line of 501 characters is refused.
- AC18 (R29, R30). Given the note is closed, then it cannot be reopened or closed again, and neither Sơn nor Linh can reply to it in Bisou; the pick and the line change only as in R33.

## 7. In the journal

A closed note becomes part of the couple's shared story: it moves to the journal feed as an entry that belongs to both of them, who can both edit or delete it.

Example: from 20:15 on 13 November both journal feeds show, under 13 November, an entry at 20:15 marked "Lời nhắn nhỏ" with both names: the four parts as sent, with Sơn's 06:35 fix, then under Linh's name "Mình muốn nói trực tiếp" and the line. Lời nhắn nhỏ no longer shows the note for either. Nobody gets "Sơn vừa viết nhật ký". At 20:30 Linh changes her line, and Sơn sees the new line; either could also delete the entry, after confirming, for both.

- R32. When the recipient closes a note, it becomes at once a journal entry in the couple's feed, shown with both partners' names (the writer's with the time, the recipient's over the pick and the line), dated the day it was closed by the Vietnam calendar and timed when it was closed, marked "Lời nhắn nhỏ", holding the four parts as sent and, under the recipient's display name, the pick and the line; it leaves Lời nhắn nhỏ for both (G3, S11; [journal](journal.md) R26, R27).
- R33. From then the [journal](journal.md) rules apply, with the exception [journal](journal.md) R26 makes to R10 and R11: both partners see it, and either may change its four parts under R2 to R5, the recipient's pick and line (R27) or its day, or delete it after confirming, when it is gone for both for good (S11).
- R34. The entry never gets photos (G5, S11); a changed pick or line keeps showing under the recipient's name, and the pick stays one of the three in R27.
- R35. A note becoming a journal entry sends no notification to anyone (G3).
- R36. Unsent, withdrawn, waiting and opened but not closed notes never reach the journal (G3).
- R37. Bisou never counts or lists closed notes: they appear only as entries in the journal feed, and no screen shows how many notes either partner has sent or received, beyond the waiting count in R19.

- AC19 (R32). Given Linh closes the note at 20:15 on 13 November, then both feeds show under 13 November an entry with both names at 20:15 marked "Lời nhắn nhỏ" with the four parts as sent and, under Linh's name, "Mình muốn nói trực tiếp" and the line; Lời nhắn nhỏ no longer shows it.
- AC20 (R33, R34). Given that entry, then Sơn and Linh can each change "Mình mong", Linh's pick, her line or the day, and each can delete it after confirming, gone for both; nobody can add a photo.
- AC21 (R35). Given both have the partner's journal kind on, then neither gets a notification when the entry appears.
- AC22 (R36, R37). Given Sơn has an unsent note and withdrew another, then neither is in the journal; no screen shows a total of notes sent or received.

## 8. Notifications

The recipient is told once when a note arrives and reminded once if it stays unopened for 3 days. No notification says what the note is about.

Example: Linh has the partner's journal kind on. The note sent at 06:30 reaches Linh at once, so her phone shows it at 06:30: "Sơn có một lời nhắn nhỏ" ("Sơn left you a kind note"); tapping it opens Lời nhắn nhỏ. Linh opens the note at 20:00, so no reminder comes. Had Linh not opened it, at 07:00 on 16 November Linh would get "Lời nhắn nhỏ của Sơn vẫn ở đây" ("Sơn's kind note is still here"), and nothing after.

- R38. When a note is sent, each device of the recipient with the partner's journal kind on gets "Sơn có một lời nhắn nhỏ" / "Sơn left you a kind note" at any hour ([notifications](notifications.md) R3, R34) (D8, G6, A1).
- R39. A note still unopened 3 days after it was sent gets one reminder, "Lời nhắn nhỏ của Sơn vẫn ở đây" / "Sơn's kind note is still here", at 07:00 on the third Vietnam day after the send day; there is never a second reminder ([notifications](notifications.md) R35) (D8).
- R40. Messages due at the same moment combine into one with the count: "Sơn có 2 lời nhắn nhỏ" / "Sơn left you 2 kind notes" for arrivals held unsent at deploy time, "2 lời nhắn nhỏ của Sơn vẫn ở đây" / "2 kind notes from Sơn are still here" for reminders. When a reminder falls due at the same 07:00 as a new arrival from the same writer, they join that arrival message as a count, and the note counts as reminded.
- R41. No notification ever contains a part, the pick or the line; tapping one opens Lời nhắn nhỏ (A2, G6).
- R42. The writer gets no notification when the 8 hours end, or when the note is opened or closed (S15).
- R43. A note opened or withdrawn before its message goes out is left out, and nothing is sent if none is left.

- AC23 (R38). Given Sơn sends at 06:30, then Linh gets "Sơn có một lời nhắn nhỏ" at 06:30; given Sơn sends at 15:00, then Linh gets it at 15:00.
- AC24 (R39). Given the note sent at 06:30 on 13 November stays unopened, then Linh gets one reminder at 07:00 on 16 November and nothing after; given Linh opened it on 13 November, then no reminder comes; given a note sent at 15:00 on 13 November stays unopened, then its reminder comes at 07:00 on 16 November.
- AC25 (R40). Given Sơn sends two notes at 06:30 and 06:45, then Linh gets "Sơn có một lời nhắn nhỏ" at each time; reminders due together count, "2 lời nhắn nhỏ của Sơn vẫn ở đây".
- AC26 (R41, R42). Given any note notification, then it shows no part, pick or line, and tapping it opens Lời nhắn nhỏ; Sơn gets nothing at 06:30, at 20:00 or at 20:15.
- AC27 (R38, R39). Given Linh never turned notifications on or turned the partner's journal kind off, then no arrival message or reminder comes, and the Home tile still shows.

## 9. What the partner can never see

A note only helps if the recipient meets the reread version, when ready. These rules hold everywhere in Bisou.

Example: from 22:30 on 12 November to 06:30 on 13 November nothing Linh sees in Bisou differs because Sơn's note exists. After 06:30 Linh never sees "lúc nào cũng", the hint Sơn saw, or that Sơn changed anything.

- R44. Before a note is sent, the recipient learns nothing of it: no tile, count, notification or change anywhere in Bisou.
- R45. Before the recipient opens a note, nothing of its parts reaches the recipient in any way, on the phone, in a notification or anywhere in Bisou.
- R46. The recipient never sees an unsent or withdrawn note, the hints the writer saw, or what the writer changed before the recipient opened it.
- R47. An outsider learns nothing of a couple's notes.

- AC28 (R44, R46). Given Sơn's note between 22:30 and 06:30, then nothing Linh sees differs; after sending, and after Sơn's 06:35 fix, Linh never sees the earlier wording, the hint or that it changed.
- AC29 (R45). Given the note waits, then Linh cannot read any part, by any means in Bisou, before tapping "Mình sẵn sàng nghe".
- AC30 (R47). Given Sơn and Linh's notes, then an outsider learns nothing of them.

## 10. Empty states and offline

The section is always there for a partner in a couple. As on every other screen ([installed app](pwa.md) R9 to R11), what was loaded stays visible, and every action needs a connection.

Example: before 22:30 on 12 November both see Lời nhắn nhỏ empty, with "Viết cho Linh" for Sơn and "Viết cho Sơn" for Linh. At 20:00 on 13 November Linh is on a train without signal and taps "Mình sẵn sàng nghe"; Bisou says it needs a connection, the note stays unopened and Sơn still sees "Chưa mở".

- R48. Lời nhắn nhỏ has two groups, "Gửi cho mình" ("To me": waiting and opened notes) and "Mình đã viết" ("I wrote": unsent, not opened and opened notes); an empty group is left out; with both empty it reads "Chưa có lời nhắn nào" ("No notes yet") and offers to write to the partner by name ("Viết cho Linh" / "Write to Linh").
- R49. The invitation to write says a note suits things that are hard to say, and that a serious problem is better talked about directly.
- R50. Without a connection, Lời nhắn nhỏ and the Home tile keep showing what was loaded, while the offline toast shows.
- R51. Saving, changing, sending, withdrawing, deleting, opening and closing a note need a connection; offline, the action says so and nothing happens later on its own.

- AC31 (R48, R49). Given no notes, then Sơn sees "Chưa có lời nhắn nào", "Viết cho Linh" and the invitation; Linh sees "Viết cho Sơn".
- AC32 (R50, R51). Given Linh is offline with the note waiting, when Linh taps "Mình sẵn sàng nghe", then Linh is told it needs a connection, the note stays unopened and Sơn still sees "Chưa mở".
- AC33 (R51). Given Sơn is offline at 06:30, when Sơn taps send, then Sơn is told it needs a connection, the note stays unsent, and it is not sent later on its own.

## 11. Personal data

A note holds feelings about the relationship. Bisou keeps it only to bring it from the writer to the recipient, and then as a journal entry.

Example: from 22:30 Bisou keeps Sơn's four parts, when the note was first saved, that it was sent, and from 20:00 that it was opened; from 20:15 the note and Linh's pick and line are a journal entry.

- R52. A note's parts are the writer's personal data; Bisou keeps them with when the note was first saved, whether it was sent and whether it was opened, only to show it to the writer, to deliver it to the recipient as these rules allow, and to show the writer "Chưa mở" or "Đã mở".
- R53. The pick and the line are the recipient's personal data, kept only in the closed note's journal entry.
- R54. No outside service reads, scores or rewrites a note: hints come only from the fixed word list in R5.
- R55. While the couple exists, Bisou keeps a note until it is deleted, withdrawn or closed; a closed note is then kept and deleted as a journal entry ([journal](journal.md) R11, R17).
- R56. The writer can read their notes at any time, change them until the recipient opens it, delete them before sending and withdraw them until opened; the recipient sees a note only once opened; after closing, R33 and R34 apply to both partners.
- R57. Notes join export, leaving the couple and account deletion when those arrive ([pairing](pairing.md), out of scope); this spec promises nothing more until then.

## Decisions to confirm

Items 1 to 9 of the first review are answered (spec review answers 11 to 15). New:

## Glossary

- **Kind note** (lời nhắn nhỏ): four short parts from one partner to the other about one issue.
- **Writer** (người viết) and **recipient** (người nhận).
- **Cool-down** (thời gian lắng lại): the 8 hours before a note can be sent.
- **Reread** (đọc lại): the step where the writer sends, with the question in R11.
- **Unsent** (chưa gửi), **not opened yet** (chưa mở), **opened** (đã mở), **closed** (đã khép lại).
- **Withdraw** (rút lại): the writer taking back a sent note before it is opened.
- **Note entry** (bài từ lời nhắn nhỏ): the journal entry a closed note becomes, shared by both partners (R32 to R34).
- **Pick** (lựa chọn khép lại): one of the three answers in R27.
- **Inbox tile** (hộp thư): the Home tile in R19.

## Out of scope

- Time capsule letters: dropped, nothing of them ships (G9).
- A weight label such as "chuyện nhỏ" (G1); a compliments step (D10); any link with the garden (D11).
- Replies, threads, reactions or comments on a note (D12); photos, voice or video (G5); drafts beyond unsent notes; Bisou rewriting a note.
- Notes to oneself or to both partners; a list, history or count of closed notes (R37).
- Export, leaving the couple and account deletion (R57).
