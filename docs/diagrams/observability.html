Alerting

notes runs every minute. Daily slots run at 07:00, 08:00, 20:00 and 03:00 Vietnam time. 12

Configured slots ping the base URL on success or /fail on failure. A missed ping triggers a failure after the 10-minute grace period. 7 18 17
